## Setup

Bar across the upper back, hands just outside the shoulders, feet about shoulder-width with toes turned out a little.

## Cues

- Big breath into the belly, brace like you're about to be pushed.
- Sit down between your heels, knees tracking over your toes.
- Hips and chest rise together on the way up.

## Common mistakes

- Knees caving in on the way up: push them out over the little toes.
- Hips shooting up first, turning the squat into a good morning.
- Cutting depth as the weight gets heavy. Same depth on every rep.

## Why runners do it

Stronger quads and glutes absorb the load of every stride, and heavy squats cost far less running time than they give back.
