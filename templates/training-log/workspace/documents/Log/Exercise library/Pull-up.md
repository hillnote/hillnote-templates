## Cues

- Start from a full hang with the shoulders engaged, not shrugged up to the ears.
- Pull the elbows down to your back pockets.
- Lower under control, all the way.

## Progressions

- Can't do one yet: slow lowering from the top (5 seconds), or a band for assistance.
- Easy: add weight with a belt or a dumbbell between the feet.

## For runners

Upper-back strength keeps posture tall when you're tired.
