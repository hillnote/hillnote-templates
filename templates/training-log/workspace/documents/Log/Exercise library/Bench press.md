## Setup

Eyes under the bar, feet flat, shoulder blades pinched back and down. Grip a little wider than the shoulders.

## Cues

- Bend the bar: it keeps the elbows at about 45 degrees.
- Touch the lower chest, then press back up towards your face.
- Keep the shoulder blades pinned for the whole set.

## Common mistakes

- Elbows flared out wide at 90 degrees.
- Bouncing the bar off the chest.
- Feet dancing about. Plant them.

## For runners

Not essential, but upper-body strength helps posture and arm drive late in a race, and it keeps the strength work balanced.
