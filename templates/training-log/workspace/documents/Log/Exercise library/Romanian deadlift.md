## Cues

- Start standing, soft knees, and push the hips back as if closing a car door with your bum.
- Bar slides down the thighs; stop when the hips stop moving back.
- Keep the back flat and the neck in line.

## Common mistakes

- Turning it into a squat by bending the knees more and more.
- Chasing depth with a rounded back.

## For runners

Long, strong hamstrings are among the best protection against the hamstring strains that come with faster running.
