## Cues

- Stand on one foot on the edge of a step, holding something for balance.
- Lower slowly until the heel is well below the step, pause, then rise as high as you can.
- Three seconds down, one up.

## Progressions

Add a dumbbell in the hand on the working side, or do them with a bent knee to work the deeper calf muscle.

## For runners

Calves and Achilles take several times bodyweight on every stride. Strong calves are among the best defences against Achilles and calf problems.
