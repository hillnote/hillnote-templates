## Setup

Bar over the middle of the foot, shins close. Hinge down, grip just outside the knees, and pull the slack out of the bar before it leaves the floor.

## Cues

- Push the floor away rather than pulling the bar up.
- Keep the bar touching the legs all the way.
- Finish tall: squeeze the glutes, don't lean back.

## Common mistakes

- Bar drifting forward away from the shins.
- Jerking the bar off the floor instead of taking the slack out first.
- Rounding the lower back as the set gets tired. End the set there.

## Why runners do it

The whole back of the body, hamstrings and glutes especially, gets stronger, which is what drives you up hills and holds your form late in a race.
