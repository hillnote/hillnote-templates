## Setup

Rear foot on a bench behind you, front foot about a long stride ahead, a dumbbell in each hand.

## Cues

- Drop straight down, back knee towards the floor.
- Front heel stays planted; drive up through it.
- A slight forward lean is fine; more lean means more glutes.

## Common mistakes

- Front foot too close, so the heel lifts.
- Rushing. Control the way down.

## For runners

Running is a series of single-leg landings. This trains exactly that, and shows up any left-right imbalance.
