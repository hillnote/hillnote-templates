## Setup

Bar on the front of the shoulders, grip just outside them, elbows slightly in front of the bar.

## Cues

- Squeeze the glutes and brace, so the lower back doesn't arch.
- Move the head back as the bar passes, then push it through underneath.
- Finish with the bar over the middle of the foot.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back to turn it into an incline bench press.
- Pressing the bar forward around the face instead of straight up.

## For runners

Builds a strong, stable trunk and shoulders. Keep it to three sets so it doesn't eat into recovery.
