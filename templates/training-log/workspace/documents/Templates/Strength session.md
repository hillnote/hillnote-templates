# Strength session

Under an hour: warm up, two main lifts, two accessories, done. Log the top set of each main lift in the Lift log afterwards so the charts stay current.

## Warm-up (10 minutes)

- [ ] 5 minutes on a bike or rower
- [ ] Hip and ankle mobility from the Wednesday session
- [ ] Ramp-up sets: empty bar × 10, then about 50%, 70% and 85% of the work weight for 5, 3 and 1 reps
