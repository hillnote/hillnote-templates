## What counts as a PR

- Same standard every time: full depth on squats, a touch on the chest on bench, lockout on deadlifts.
- Estimated maxes from sets of six reps or fewer. The formulas get less reliable above that.
- A PR on a bad night's sleep still counts. A PR with a sloppy rep doesn't.
