# What is Hillnote?

![Pasted image](resources/images/Start%20here/1780902503781.webp)**Hillnote is a workspace tool** that is built to capture your work in AI native formats. It focuses on 3 key pillars

1. Ensure you have the **best experience crafting your thoughts** in the workspace with a blend of the right interfaces, formats and local AI tools.
2. Making sure your **workspace is ready to integrate with all tools** using universal formats and overarching context files to keep the goal of the workspace available across.
3. Keep things **fair, extendable and ready for scale.**
