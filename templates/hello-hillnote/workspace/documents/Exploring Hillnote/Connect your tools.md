## Connect your tools

While a more extensive set of guides are available in [hillnote.com/connect](https://hillnote.com/connect) there are 2 easy methods to integrate your workspace with your AI tools.

:::tabs
:::tab "Via MCP"
**Fairly straightforward** - **connect to your tools via the local or cloud MCP’s(you will need to sync the workspace to access it via cloud MCP’s).** All the instructions you need are in setting > MCP. Some apps call them “Connectors”, “Plugins” or “connections

![Pasted image](resources/images/Start%20here/1779091205748.webp)
:::endtab
:::tab "Directly via Files"
**You could alternatively choose to open the workspace folder directly within the app** of your choice and start working there. All context needed by the AI tools are available here.  
![Pasted image](resources/images/Start%20here/1779091334858.webp)
:::endtab
:::endtabs
