# Release notes

Customer-facing notes, newest first. Each entry says what changed and why it matters, in the customer's words rather than ours.

## 4.8 · Saved dashboards (beta)

Save any dashboard's filters, grouping and date range under a name and reopen it in one click from the home screen. Filters now also survive switching tabs.

*In beta with a few accounts; everyone gets it soon.*

## 4.7 · Faster dashboards at peak times

The busiest dashboards now load in under 2.5 seconds on Monday mornings, down from almost 10 for our largest accounts. Nothing to switch on.

## 4.6 · Onboarding checklist

New accounts now see a five-step checklist on the home screen, from connecting data to sharing a first dashboard. Most new accounts finish it on day one.

## 4.5 · Dashboard templates

Ten ready-made operational dashboards: on-time rate, jobs at risk, driver hours, SLA breaches by region and more. Pick one, connect your data, done.
