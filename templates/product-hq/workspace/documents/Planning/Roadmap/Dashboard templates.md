Ten ready-made operational dashboards: on-time rate, jobs at risk, driver hours and more.

**The bet:** with a template to start from, new accounts get a live dashboard on day 1 (34% to 50%).

**What happened:** partial. Median time to first dashboard view fell from 41s to 28s, which nobody predicted. Day-1 live dashboards only moved from 34% to 37% before the onboarding checklist shipped, so the templates alone didn't do it.

**Learned:** templates help people who already know what they want. New accounts need to be walked to one.
