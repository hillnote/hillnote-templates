Email a PDF of any dashboard on a schedule.

**The bet:** people who never log in get value from a Monday PDF, and weekly retention rises 5 points.

**What happened:** missed. 11% of schedules were still active after a month, and retention didn't move.

**Learned:** nobody opens an attachment. People wanted three numbers in the body of the email. That became Alert digests.
