# Customer interview: Brightwater Logistics

**Who:** Head of operations and one senior dispatcher · **Segment:** Mid-market, 140 people · **Customer for:** 7 months · **Interviewer:** Product + Design

## Context

Brightwater runs same-day courier routes across two cities. They moved from spreadsheets to ${product} seven months ago and use it every day, but usage drops sharply after Tuesday. We wanted to understand why.

## Key moments

- **The Monday rebuild.** The ops lead rebuilds the same view every Monday for the 10:00 ops meeting, then screenshots it into a slide. Twenty minutes, every week. Logged as [[Rebuilding the same view every Monday|doc:documents/Discovery/Feedback/Rebuilding%20the%20same%20view%20every%20Monday.md]].
- **Tabs lose state.** The dispatcher keeps two tabs open and loses filters when switching. Logged as [[Filters reset when switching tabs|doc:documents/Discovery/Feedback/Filters%20reset%20when%20switching%20tabs.md]].
- **Three numbers.** "On-time rate, jobs at risk, and drivers over hours. That's it. Everything else is detail."

## Jobs to be done

1. When the week starts, show me where we are slipping so I can move people before it costs us.
2. When a job is about to breach, tell me early enough to reassign it.

## Opportunities

| Opportunity | Evidence | Initiative |
|---|---|---|
| Saved, shareable views | Monday rebuild, tab resets | Saved dashboards |
| Always-visible KPIs | "Three numbers" | KPI pins |
| Monday summary by email | Usage drops after Tuesday | Alert digests |
