## Questions

Ask about what they did in the past, never about what they would do with your idea.

1. Tell me about the last time this happened. What happened, step by step?
2. What did you do about it? Can you show me?
3. What did it cost you, in money or time?
4. What have you tried to fix it? Why did you stop?
5. Who else is involved when this happens?
