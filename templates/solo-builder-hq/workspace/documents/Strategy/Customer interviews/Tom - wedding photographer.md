**Who:** wedding and portrait photographer, 25 weddings a year, books a year ahead.

> "Deposits are the whole game. I need 30 percent up front and the rest two weeks before the wedding."

**What he does today:** Slipsend for each invoice and a note in his phone for who has paid the deposit.

**Pains**

- Two invoices per job, months apart, and nothing linking them
- Couples who "forgot" the final payment
- Ads on his invoices from the free tool

**What would make him switch:** deposits and scheduled final payments. That is not in the MVP.

**Decision:** keep photographers in the segment, but deposits wait until after launch. They're first on the out-of-scope list in the [[MVP spec|doc:documents/Build/MVP%20spec.md]].
