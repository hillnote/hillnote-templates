# Announcement drafts

One message, four shapes. Each channel gets its own first line, because people can smell a cross-post. Final versions are due a week before launch (see the [[Launch checklist|doc:documents/Launch/Launch%20checklist.md]]).

The facts every draft agrees on: **${product_name}**, $${price_monthly} a month, a 14-day trial with no card, founding members keep their price, and the promise: invoices that chase themselves.

:::tabs
:::tab "Launch email"
**Subject:** Harbor is open, and your price is locked

Hi {first name},

A few weeks ago you joined a waitlist for an invoicing app that chases late payments for you. It's ready.

**What it does**

- Sends an invoice in under a minute
- Puts a card payment link on every invoice
- Follows up on the due date, at a week and at two weeks, in a tone you pick, and stops the moment you're paid

You can start a 14-day trial today, no card needed. After that it's $12 a month.

One request: when you send your first reminder, reply and tell me if it sounded like you. That's the part I care about most.

Ines

Freelance designer here. Last spring I had four invoices more than 30 days late at once, and I spent a whole Friday writing "just following up" emails that I rewrote three times each.

So I spent ten weeks building Harbor. You send an invoice, your client pays by card from a link, and if they don't, Harbor follows up on a schedule in a tone you choose: friendly, firm or final. It stops the moment they pay.

It's $12 a month with a 14-day trial. I'd love blunt feedback, especially on the reminder emails. What would make you trust a tool to email your clients?

## Before anything goes out

- [ ] Each community post has its own first line and its own tracked link
- [ ] Prices and trial length match the [[Landing page copy|doc:documents/Launch/Landing%20page%20copy.md]]
- [ ] No claims without numbers behind them ("paid twice as fast" waits until the data says so)
- [ ] Read every draft aloud once. If it sounds like an ad, cut a sentence.
