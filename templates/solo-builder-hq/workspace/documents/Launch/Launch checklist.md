# Launch checklist

Launch day is the **Tuesday of week 10**, three weeks from now. A launch is not one day; it's three weeks of quiet preparation, one loud day and one week of listening. Tick things off here; the Launch milestone on the Build board holds the bigger pieces of work.

## Three weeks out (this week)

- [x] Waitlist page live with email capture
- [x] Founding-member offer sent to the waitlist
- [x] Launch day on the Build board calendar
- [ ] Beta invites out to 12 freelancers ([[card|doc:documents/Build/Build%20board/Private%20beta%20with%2012%20freelancers.md]])
- [ ] Decide the one number that means the launch worked (proposal: 60 trials in the first week)

## Two weeks out

- [ ] A check-in with each beta user after their first reminder goes out
- [ ] Four help articles: connecting payouts, a first invoice, how reminders work, cancelling
- [ ] Terms, privacy policy and refund policy published
- [ ] Support inbox with five saved replies
- [ ] Error alerts and an uptime check that pages your phone

## One week out

- [ ] Launch landing page live with beta quotes ([[Landing page copy|doc:documents/Launch/Landing%20page%20copy.md]])
- [ ] [[Announcement drafts|doc:documents/Launch/Announcement%20drafts.md]] final for every channel
- [ ] A tracked link per channel, so you know which post worked
- [ ] One real payment in live mode with your own card, then refund it
- [ ] Ask five beta users if they'd share a sentence on launch day

## Launch day

- [ ] 8:00 Founding members get the email first, with a thank-you
- [ ] 9:00 The waitlist email
- [ ] 10:00 First community post; space the others through the day
- [ ] Reply to every comment and email within two hours
- [ ] No deploys after 14:00 unless something is broken
- [ ] Evening: a launch entry in the [[Ship log|doc:documents/Build/Ship%20log.md]] and the numbers in the Money sheet

## The week after

- [ ] Email every trial on day 3: "What almost stopped you?"
- [ ] Talk to three people who signed up and never sent an invoice
- [ ] Run `/pressure_test` with the launch numbers and update the [[Lean canvas|doc:documents/Strategy/Lean%20canvas.md]]

## Numbers to watch on the day

| Number | Why | Good sign |
|---|---|---|
| Trials started | Did the message land | 60 in week one |
| Trials that send an invoice in 24 hours | Did onboarding work | Half of them |
| Payments through the link | Assumption 3 | Any, on day one |
| Support emails | Assumption 6 | Fewer than trials ÷ 10 |
| Errors | Did anything break | Zero you heard about from a customer |
