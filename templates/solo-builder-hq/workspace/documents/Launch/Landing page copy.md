## How it works

1. **Send.** Pick a client, add what you did, send. Under a minute.
2. **Get paid.** Every invoice carries a card payment link. Your client doesn't need an account.
3. **Let it follow up.** Friendly on the due date, firm a week later, final after two. It stops the moment they pay.

## Why freelancers switch

**No more awkward emails.** Pick a tone once. ${product_name} writes and sends the follow-ups, from your name, and you approve the first one before it goes.

**One number.** What you're owed, what's overdue and who owes it, on one screen.

**Built for one person.** No accounting, no setup, nothing to learn. Your accountant gets clean PDFs.

## In their words

Until the beta quotes arrive, the page uses interview quotes, with permission:

> "I send the invoice, then I wait, then I feel awkward, then I wait more." (a brand designer)

> "Show me one number: what I'm owed. Then go get it." (a motion designer)

## Pricing

**One plan: $${price_monthly} a month.** Unlimited invoices and clients, card payments and automatic reminders. Founding members keep this price for life. #pricing

Card fees are charged by the payment provider and shown on every payout.

## Questions people ask

**Will reminders annoy my clients?** You choose the tone and the schedule, and you see the first one before it's sent. Reminders stop the moment an invoice is paid.

**Do my clients need an account?** No. They click the link and pay.

**Can my clients pay by bank transfer?** Yes. Mark the invoice paid by hand and the reminders stop.

**Does it do my accounting?** No, on purpose. It gets you paid, and gives your accountant clean PDFs.

**What if I leave?** Download every invoice as a PDF, any time.

## Last call to action

**Stop chasing. Start free.** Same button as the hero.
