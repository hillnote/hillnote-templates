# References

Where the ${app_name} v3 direction came from. Each reference earns its place by giving us one specific thing, and each one also has a part we deliberately leave behind. If a reference can't answer "what do we take?", it goes.

The palette, type and mood these produced live on the [[Fernway moodboard|doc:documents/Exploration/Fernway%20moodboard.moodboard.md]].

## Old mail-order seed catalogues

One plant per spread, a plain-language care note under each picture, generous margins.

**Take**

- One plant per card on Home, with a single line of care below the name
- Sentence-case labels that read like advice ("Likes a bright windowsill")

**Leave**

- Ornamental borders and ten-word headlines
- Prices and urgency ("Order now!")

## Botanical illustration plates

Muted colours with one accent, and labelled parts with thin leader lines.

**Take**

- The restrained palette: Paper, Ink and Fern, with Terracotta saved for the one thing that needs attention
- Labelled call-outs for the photo diagnosis ("yellowing starts at the lower leaves")

**Leave**

- Latin-only labels; the common name comes first, the botanical name second and smaller

## Weather apps' hourly strip

A horizontal strip you scan in a second, with detail one tap away.

**Take**

- The seven-day strip at the top of the [[Watering calendar|doc:documents/System/Components/Watering%20calendar.md]]
- Icons that always come with a label, never colour alone

**Leave**

- Animated backgrounds and decorative motion

## Garden-centre plant tags

Light, water and size on a card the size of a thumb.

**Take**

- The icon row on the [[Plant card|doc:documents/System/Components/Plant%20card.md]]: light, water and pet safety
- The habit of putting the one number that matters in bold ("every 7 days")

**Leave**

- Laminated gloss, tiny type and every symbol at once

## Field guides

Calm, careful language about uncertainty: "often confused with", "check the underside".

**Take**

- The diagnosis voice: "This looks like overwatering. Here's how to check." See [[Voice and microcopy|doc:documents/System/Voice%20and%20microcopy.md]].
- A "how to check" step for every result

**Leave**

- Exhaustive keys and taxonomies; people want one next step, not a decision tree

## Medication reminder apps

One-tap confirmation and snoozes that respect the person.

**Take**

- "Done" as the largest target in the [[Care reminder banner|doc:documents/System/Components/Care%20reminder%20banner.md]]
- Three snooze choices at most

**Leave**

- Alarm-style urgency, red badges and streak guilt
