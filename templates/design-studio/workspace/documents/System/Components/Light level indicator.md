Shows how much light a spot gets compared with what the plant likes, using the phone's light sensor or a quick photo. Still an experiment: we don't know yet whether people trust a reading from their phone.

**Open questions**

- [ ] Do we show lux numbers, or only "low, medium, bright"?
- [ ] How do we say a reading is rough without sounding unsure of everything?
- [ ] v3 or v3.1? On the agenda for Thursday's review.

**Directions on the table**

1. A three-step scale (low, medium, bright) with the plant's sweet spot marked
2. A sun icon that fills up, with words underneath
3. No indicator, just a sentence on the [[Plant card|doc:documents/System/Components/Plant%20card.md]] ("Likes a brighter spot")

**Accessibility**

- Any scale needs labels on every step, not just a gradient
