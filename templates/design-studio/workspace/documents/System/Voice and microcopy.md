# Voice and microcopy

${app_name} talks like a friend who happens to know a lot about plants: calm, specific and kind. It never nags, never guilts, and never pretends to know more than it does. #microcopy

## Principles

1. **Calm.** No exclamation marks, no countdowns, no red-alert words. A thirsty plant is not an emergency.
2. **Specific.** Name the plant, the care and the time. "Water your monstera today" beats "Your plants need attention".
3. **Kind.** Plants die; it's not a moral failing. We never say "you forgot" or "you missed".
4. **Honest about uncertainty.** Diagnosis results say "looks like" and always come with a way to check.
5. **Brief.** If a word can go, it goes. Every string has a character limit in the UX copy sheet.

## Say this, not that

| Say | Not | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your monstera is thirsty. Water it today? | You forgot to water your monstera! | Kind, specific, no guilt |
| Watered. Next drink in 6 days. | Great job!!! Task completed | Calm; tells you what happens next |
| This looks like overwatering. Here's how to check. | Your plant has root rot. | Honest about confidence |
| Add a plant | Get started on your plant journey | Verb plus noun, nothing extra |
| Skip for now | No thanks, I don't care about my plants | Never shame a choice |
| Overdue by 2 days | URGENT | One word of colour, one fact |

## Patterns

**Notifications**: plant, need, time. At most 60 characters, so the whole thing fits on a lock screen.

- "Your fern would like a drink this evening."
- "Time to feed the calathea. It's been a month."

**Buttons**: verb first, sentence case, at most 18 characters. The same action has the same label everywhere: always "Add a plant", never "New plant" on one screen and "Add" on another.

**Empty states**: what will appear here, then one action.

- "No plants yet. Add your first one: a photo is enough."

**Errors**: what happened, then what to do. Never blame the person and never show a code.

- "We couldn't read that photo. Try again in brighter light."

**Diagnosis**: a likely cause, how sure we are in plain words, one way to check, one thing to do.

- "This looks like overwatering. Here's how to check."

**Celebrations**: small and rare. A new leaf gets one line in Bloom, no confetti.
