> "I don't know the pot size. I just want it to tell me when to water."

Round 1: five of six participants stalled on the pot size question, and two guessed. It sat at question 7 of 12, before the app had shown anything useful.

**Fix shipped in the prototype:** onboarding is now three steps (snap or pick a plant, where it lives, first reminder). Pot size and soil moved to the plant profile and are optional. Round 2: only one participant called onboarding long.

**Goal:** first plant in under a minute, from the [[Project brief|doc:documents/Briefs/Project%20brief.md]].
