In the week 3 crit, Jules pointed out that tall plant photos pushed the name under the image on small phones, so "Monstera" became "Monst" behind a leaf.

**Fix:** the name and care line never sit on the photo. The photo is a fixed square above them, and long names wrap to two lines.
