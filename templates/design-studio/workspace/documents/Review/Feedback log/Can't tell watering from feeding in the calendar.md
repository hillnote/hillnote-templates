> "Are the blue ones water? Then what are the green ones? Also water?"

Three of six participants couldn't tell watering days from feeding days in the week strip. The only difference was the colour of the dot, which also fails WCAG 1.4.1 (use of colour).

**Proposed fix:** a drop icon for water and a leaf icon for feeding, each with a count, and a short legend the first time someone opens the calendar.
