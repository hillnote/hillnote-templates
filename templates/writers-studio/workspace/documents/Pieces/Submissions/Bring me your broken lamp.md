**Subject:** Pitch: three repair cafés, one Saturday each

Hello,

At quarter to ten on the first Saturday of the month, a queue forms outside St Anne's church hall. A man holds a brass lamp the way you'd hold a baby at a christening. By one o'clock, 29 of the 41 broken things carried in that morning will go home working.

I'd like to write 2,500 words for Slow Craft Quarterly on repair cafés: who runs them, what people bring, and why a room full of strangers fixing toasters feels more like a festival than a chore. I'd visit three cafés, starting with Femi Adeyemi's Fix-it Saturday, which began when his own radio came back marked "beyond economic repair".
