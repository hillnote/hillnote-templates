## The rules

- Unpublished essays only, up to 3,000 words. A chapter of a book in progress is allowed if it hasn't appeared anywhere.
- Anonymous judging: no name on the manuscript, and nothing in the text that gives it away.

## The cut

Keep the scene, the craft and the man with his late wife's shoes. Drop the backstory and the widening. End on the ticket: "He tears the ticket in half and gives me the half with the number on it."
