Issue 43, an explainer. Readers keep asking what "right to repair" actually means, and every answer they find is written for lawyers or campaigners.

**Structure:** Explainer: the question, the short answer, the ladder, what it means for you, what we still don't know.

## The ladder

1. What is the right to repair? Short answer: being able to get the parts, tools and information to fix what you own, or to have someone else fix it.
2. Why would anyone be against it?
3. What has actually changed, and where?
4. What does it mean for a phone, a washing machine, a car?
5. What can I do?

## Watch out

- Rules differ by country and are changing quickly. Every factual claim gets a source in **Reading notes** and a "check before sending" mark.
- Keep it under 1,800 words. If it runs over, it becomes two issues.
