Issue 41. How my grandmother darned socks, how I learned to do it from a library book thirty years later, and why a darn is the most patient thing you can do with your hands.

**Preheader:** It takes twenty minutes, and it will outlast the sock.

## Opening

My grandmother kept a wooden mushroom in her sewing tin. For years I thought it was a toy. It was a darning mushroom: you slide it inside the heel of a sock so the hole sits stretched over the dome, and then you weave, under and over, until the hole is a small square of new cloth.
