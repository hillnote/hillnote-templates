# Newsletter plan

**${newsletter_name}** is a fortnightly letter about repair, care and the slow work of making things last, written by ${pen_name} and sent every other Friday morning. About 2,100 readers, most of whom found it through a friend.

## What every issue does

One idea, one story, one thing to try. If an issue has two ideas, it is two issues.

## Formats

| Format | Length | How often |
|---|---|---|
| Essay | 1,200 to 1,600 words | Most issues |
| Mailbag | 800 to 1,000 words | Every sixth issue: reader stories, lightly edited |
| Explainer | 1,400 to 1,800 words | When a reader question keeps coming back |
| Field note | 400 to 600 words | When the essay isn't ready. Twice a quarter at most |

## Subject lines

Under 50 characters, no capitals for emphasis, no questions you wouldn't ask a friend. Run `/headline_options` for ten candidates, built on the patterns in the [[Structure cheat-sheets|doc:documents/Craft/Structure%20cheat-sheets.md#headlines]].

## Welcome email

New readers get one email, not a sequence:

> Thank you for subscribing to ${newsletter_name}. Every other Friday you'll get one essay about repair, care, or the people who still fix things. If something in an issue makes you want to fix something, reply and tell me about it. I read every reply.
>
> ${pen_name}

## Sign-off

Every issue ends the same way: "Mend something this week." Then the name, and nothing else.

## Growth

One rule: reply to every reader reply within two days. The mailbag issues prove it works; they get more forwards than anything else.
