The second visit to the shop on Wren Street, about ninety minutes, recorded with permission.

**Highlights**

- Apprenticed to his uncle at fifteen; took over the shop at thirty-one.
- Resoles about forty pairs a week, down from "more than a hundred" when he started.
- Keeps a drawer of shoes that were never collected, some of them for nine years. "Someone might come."
- On glue and stitching: "Glue is a promise for this winter. Stitching is a promise for the boots' next owner."
