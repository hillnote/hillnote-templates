A lecture on the decorative arts, and the source of Morris's famous rule about useful and beautiful things.

**Notes so far**

- The rule is about houses, but the lecture is really about work: Morris argues that ugly, badly made things come from miserable working lives.
- Read it next to the Walden notes. Both want fewer, better things, for different reasons.

**Quote**

> "Have nothing in your houses that you do not know to be useful, or believe to be beautiful."
