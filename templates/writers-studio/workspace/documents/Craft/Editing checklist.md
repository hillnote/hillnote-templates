# Editing checklist

Edit in passes, from big to small. Fixing the commas in a paragraph you are about to cut is the most reliable way to lose an afternoon. Each pass has one job; don't do the next pass's job early.

Copy the checklist into the piece's page when you start editing it, and tick as you go.

## Pass 1: structure

Read the whole draft in one sitting, on a different screen or on paper. Don't fix anything yet; take notes.

- [ ] Can you say what the piece is about in one sentence? Write it at the top
- [ ] Does the opening earn the second paragraph?
- [ ] Is every section doing a job the others aren't? Cut or merge the ones that aren't
- [ ] Is the turn, where the piece changes direction, about two-thirds of the way in?
- [ ] Does the ending answer the opening, or open a better question?

## Pass 2: paragraphs

- [ ] Each paragraph has one idea, and its first sentence says what it is
- [ ] Transitions carry logic ("but", "so", "which means"), not filler ("additionally")
- [ ] Every scene has a place, a time and a body in it
- [ ] Every claim has a source in **Reading notes**, or is plainly an opinion

## Pass 3: lines

- [ ] Cut the words in the [[words to cut|doc:documents/Craft/Style%20guide.md#words-to-cut]] table
- [ ] Break any sentence over 30 words, unless it is the one long sentence the paragraph earns
- [ ] Swap passive voice for active wherever the actor matters
- [ ] Replace one abstract noun per paragraph with something you could photograph
- [ ] Run the passage through the readability check below; for the newsletter, aim for a reading ease of 60 or more

## Pass 4: proof

- [ ] Read it aloud, start to finish: the [[read-aloud test|doc:documents/Craft/Style%20guide.md#read-aloud]]
- [ ] Names, dates and numbers checked against their source
- [ ] Every link opens the right page
- [ ] Spell-check, then read the first and last paragraphs once more, slowly

## Readability check

Paste a paragraph or a whole draft to see its reading ease, long sentences, adverbs and likely passive voice. Treat it as a smoke alarm, not a judge: a low score on a deliberately long sentence is fine.

[html:Readability check](resources/html/readability-check/index.html)

## When to stop

A piece is done when another pass changes things without improving them. If you have swapped the same word back and forth twice, move the piece to **Ready**.
