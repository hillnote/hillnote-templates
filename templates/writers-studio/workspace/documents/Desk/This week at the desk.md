# This week at the desk

One page for the week: what gets written, in what order, and how much. The daily target is **${daily_words} words**, five days a week, which makes **${=daily_words * 5} words** by Friday. Words count whether or not they survive the edit.

## The shape of a day

| Time | What | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 8:30 to 9:45 | Book sprint | Door closed, no research tabs open |
| 10:00 to 11:00 | Newsletter | Draft or edit the next issue |
| Afternoon | Research and admin | Reading notes, reader replies, pitches |

The morning sprint goes to the book because the book is the thing most likely to slip. Newsletter deadlines enforce themselves; book deadlines don't.
