Kintsugi has become the only repair metaphor anyone reaches for. Collect others: boro and sashiko, darned elbows, a patched sail, a resoled boot, a re-strung chair.

**Shape:** a list issue, or a short series with one repair tradition a month. Each one needs a person who still does it, not just a photo.
