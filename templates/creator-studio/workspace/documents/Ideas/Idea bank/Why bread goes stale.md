Stale bread isn't mostly dry. Its starch slowly recrystallises, which is why a sealed loaf still goes hard, and why a few minutes in a hot oven brings it back for a while.

**Demo:** two slices from the same stale loaf, one toasted for three minutes, one not. Then the same test in the fridge, which makes bread stale *faster*.

**Why it scores high:** everyone has had it happen, and the fridge twist is a genuine surprise.
