# Script template

Duplicate this page for every long video and fill it top to bottom. The hook comes first, because it decides everything else.

**Calendar row:** link the row in the content calendar here.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Title | Under 60 characters, the curious bit first |
| Thumbnail text | Three words at most |
| Pillar | Myth or fact, Why it works, Try this at home, or Kitchen gear |
| Sponsor | None, or the brand and the beat it sits in |

## Hook (first 15 seconds)

Pick a formula from the [[hook formulas|doc:documents/Brand/Channel%20strategy.md#hook-formulas]] and write the hook word for word. Read it aloud; if it runs past 15 seconds, cut.
