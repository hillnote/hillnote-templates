## Hook (0:00 to 0:15)

> "Every recipe says salt your pasta water. Half the internet says it makes the water boil faster. I put two identical pans on two identical burners to find out, and the answer isn't what either side thinks."

**On screen:** two pans, two thermometers, a stopwatch. Salt pours in slow motion.

## Promise (0:15 to 0:30)

By the end you'll know what salt actually does to boiling water, why it *looks* like it boils faster, and how much salt your pasta really needs.

## Payoff

> "Salt doesn't make water boil faster. It makes it boil very slightly later, and it makes your pasta taste of something. Add it for flavour, not for speed."

- [x] The fizz is dissolved air and vapour forming bubbles on the salt crystals, not faster boiling.
