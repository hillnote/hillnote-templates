## Promise

By the end you'll know the four things that make cookies spread, and which one to fix first.

## Payoff

> "Cookies spread when the fat melts before the structure sets. Every fix buys the structure more time: colder butter, chilled dough, flour by weight, a hotter oven. Start with the butter."
