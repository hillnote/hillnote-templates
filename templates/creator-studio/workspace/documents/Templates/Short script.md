# Short script

A Short is one idea in under 60 seconds. If you need two ideas, make two Shorts.

| Second | Job | Script |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | **Hook**: show the thing, say the surprise |  |
| 3 to 15 | **Setup**: the question, in one sentence |  |
| 15 to 45 | **Payoff**: the demo and the answer |  |
| 45 to 60 | **Loop**: end on a shot that leads back to the first frame |  |

**On-screen text:** three words, readable at phone size, never covering the food.

**Caption:** the hook again in plain words, then one line of context. Two or three hashtags at most, such as `#kitchenscience`.

## Checklist

- [ ] Something moves in the first second
- [ ] Vertical, 9:16, subject in the middle third
- [ ] Captions burned in; most people watch with the sound off
- [ ] Posted to Shorts, TikTok and Instagram within the same day
