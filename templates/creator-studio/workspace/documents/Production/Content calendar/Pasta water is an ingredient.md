A dedicated Short for Fieldstone Pasta; the deal is in the **Sponsors** sheet.

## Script, 45 seconds

1. **Hook, 0 to 3 s:** "Stop pouring this down the sink."
2. **Setup:** pasta water is full of starch, and starch helps oil and water stay mixed.
3. **Demo:** butter and cheese with plain water splits. With pasta water it turns glossy.
4. **Payoff:** "A ladle of pasta water is the difference between greasy and silky."
5. **Loop:** end on the pour, so it runs straight back into the first frame.
