**Hook used:** "You're not crying because onions are sad. You're crying because you just set off a tiny chemical defence system."

**The science:** cutting an onion breaks open its cells, and two compounds that were kept apart meet and make a gas that irritates your eyes. Chilling the onion slows the reaction; a sharp knife breaks fewer cells.
