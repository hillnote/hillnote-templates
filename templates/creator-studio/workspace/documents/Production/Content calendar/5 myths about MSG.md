Seven slides. One claim per slide, with the evidence in a single line underneath.

1. **Cover:** "5 things people get wrong about MSG"
2. **Myth: it's artificial.** Glutamate occurs naturally in tomatoes, parmesan and mushrooms. MSG is that same glutamate with sodium attached.
3. **Myth: it causes headaches.** Controlled studies haven't found a consistent effect at normal food amounts.
4. **Myth: it's very salty.** MSG contains about a third of the sodium of table salt by weight.
5. **Myth: it's a modern invention.** It was first isolated from seaweed broth more than a century ago.
6. **Myth: chefs don't use it.** Plenty do; they call it umami.
7. **Close:** "Use it like salt: a pinch, to taste." Save this for later.
