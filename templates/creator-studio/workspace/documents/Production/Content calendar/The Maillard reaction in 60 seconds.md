## Script, 55 seconds

1. **Hook:** a pale steak next to a browned one. "Same steak. One of these tastes of nothing."
2. **Setup:** browning isn't burning. Above roughly 140 °C, sugars and proteins react and make hundreds of new flavour compounds.
3. **Payoff:** that's why a dry surface and a hot pan matter. Water keeps the surface at 100 °C, too cool to brown.
4. **Loop:** back to the two steaks.
