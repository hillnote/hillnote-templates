From the idea bank. The classic school experiment, done properly, with a thermometer in the ice.

## Draft script

1. **Hook:** shaking a bag. "Five minutes, no freezer, no machine."
2. **Setup:** salt makes ice melt at a lower temperature, so a bag of ice and salt gets far colder than ice alone.
3. **Demo:** thermometer in plain ice, then in salted ice. The number drops well below zero.
4. **Payoff:** the cream in the inner bag freezes as it's shaken.
5. **Loop:** the first spoonful, then back to the shaking.

- [ ] Test the ratio: how much salt per bag of ice
- [ ] Gloves on camera; the bag gets painfully cold
