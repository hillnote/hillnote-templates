## The test

Four identical steaks, cooked to the same core temperature:

1. Cut straight away
2. Rested 5 minutes
3. Rested 10 minutes
4. Rested 10 minutes, then flashed in the pan again

Weigh the juice on the board for each, and blind-taste all four with two guests.
