Seasoning isn't oil sitting on the pan. Heated thin layers of oil react and link together into a hard, slick coating bonded to the iron.

**Hook idea:** "Your cast iron pan is coated in a plastic you made yourself."

**Demo ideas**

- Season three pans with three different oils and test each with an egg
