# Factions

Five groups with their own goals, moving whether or not the party is watching. Each has a face in the NPCs database. Between sessions, give every faction a turn (see the bottom of this page), so the world feels alive.

## The Lamplighters' Guild

[The keepers of the light.]{#lamplighters} **Wants:** keep the Spire burning, whatever it takes. **Method:** secrecy, tradition, quiet favours. **Resources:** the Spire, the Abbey's goodwill, a fleet of tenders.

Old, proud and frightened. Most lamplighters believe the stormglass comes from a deep vein under the Teeth. Only [[Corvin Hale|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Corvin%20Hale.md]] and two senior keepers know about the bargain. [[Old Hob|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Old%20Hob.md]] saw something he shouldn't have.

## The Harbour Compact

[The merchants who run the port.]{#compact} **Wants:** safe trade, and someone to blame for the losses. **Method:** money, contracts, hired help. **Resources:** the port, its marines and its cutters.

The merchant council that runs [[Lanternfall|doc:documents/World/Places/Lanternfall.md]]. [[Odile Marr|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Odile%20Marr.md]] hired the party; Councillor [[Edda Thorne|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Edda%20Thorne.md]] is quietly profiting from the wrecks through insurance.

## The Saltborn

[The marsh folk.]{#saltborn} **Wants:** to be left alone, and to keep the Deep Bell silent forever. **Method:** salt-witchery, old songs, refusing to cooperate. **Resources:** the marsh, its paths and its memory.

Descendants of the people who survived the Ruin in the marsh. [[Mother Ysa|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Mother%20Ysa.md]] knows more about the Deep Bell than anyone alive, and trusts no one from the city.

## The Gull Court

[The smugglers under the cliffs.]{#gull-court} **Wants:** profit from every crisis. **Method:** smuggling, blackmail, charm. **Resources:** the coves at [[Cinder Quay|doc:documents/World/Places/Cinder%20Quay.md]], fast boats and informants everywhere.

[[Pell Rook|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Pell%20Rook.md]] has been smuggling stormglass for the Guildmaster without knowing what it is. Wren owes him money.

## The Drowned Choir

[The cult behind the bells.]{#choir} **Wants:** to raise Veyr. **Method:** grief, song and recruitment of the bereaved. **Resources:** the drowned, the bells, and agents in the Abbey.

[[Sister Maudlin Greaves|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Sister%20Maudlin%20Greaves.md]] recruits the families of the drowned at the [[Weeping Abbey|doc:documents/World/Places/Weeping%20Abbey.md]]; [[The Choirmother|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/The%20Choirmother.md]] waits below in [[The Sunken Stair|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Sunken%20Stair.md]].

## Faction turns

After each session, roll a d6 for each faction and move its clock on the [[Campaign arc|doc:documents/Quests/Campaign%20arc.md]]:

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Setback: something goes wrong for them, and the party might hear about it |
| 2–4 | Steady: they pursue their goal; describe one rumour the party could hear |
| 5–6 | Advance: tick their clock and make a visible move |

If the party directly helped or hindered a faction this session, skip the roll and decide.
