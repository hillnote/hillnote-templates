Sixty, silver-haired, gentle, exhausted. He has kept the light burning for twelve years by making a bargain he can't take back, and he tells himself every night that it was the right choice.

## Voice and mannerisms

- Pours tea before anyone speaks, and keeps pouring.
- Answers questions with questions: "And what would you have me do instead?"
- Rubs his thumb over a ring of dark bronze (bell metal) when he lies.

## Wants and fears

- **Wants:** the light to last one more winter, and then another.
- **Fears:** the dark coast he remembers from the Dark Night, and being found out.

## What he knows

Everything about the bargain. He doesn't know what the Choirmother really wants.

## Hooks

- Can be turned into an ally if the party offers another way to keep the light burning.
- Carries the [[Stormglass Lantern|doc:documents/Bestiary%20and%20Loot/Items/Stormglass%20Lantern.md]].
