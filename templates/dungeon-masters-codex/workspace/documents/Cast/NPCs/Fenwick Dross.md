A nervous, kind ferryman who went missing for three days and was found by the party in Session 1, half-drowned on a sandbar and singing.

## Voice and mannerisms

- Stammers, and repeats the last word you said: "The Spire? Spire."
- Won't go out on the water after dark any more.

## What he knows

- Saw lights moving under the water near the Teeth.
- Heard a choir singing his name.

## Arc

Resolved: rescued in [[Session 1 - The Drowned Bell|doc:documents/Sessions/Sessions/Session%201%20-%20The%20Drowned%20Bell.md]], which completed [[The Missing Ferryman|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/The%20Missing%20Ferryman.md]]. He'll ferry the party anywhere in daylight, free, for life. A good source of rumours.

Lives in [[Saltmarrow|doc:documents/World/Places/Saltmarrow.md]].
