Forty, handsome, missing two fingers, and never not smiling. He runs the Gull Court like a family business, which it is.

## Voice and mannerisms

- Sing-song voice; calls everyone "my duck".
- Laughs at his own threats, which makes them worse.
- Feeds gulls from his pocket during negotiations.

## Wants and fears

- **Wants:** profit, and the debt Wren owes him paid with interest.
- **Fears:** being made a fool of, in public.

## What he knows

Who has been buying "lamp oil" by the crate, and where the boats pick it up.
