The last priestess of the sea-kings, kept alive for twelve centuries by the echo of the Deep Bell. She doesn't think of herself as a villain. She is bringing her city home.

## Voice and mannerisms

- Two voices at once, a third apart, perfectly in tune.
- Calls everyone "little lamp".
- Hums whenever someone else is speaking.

## Wants and fears

- **Wants:** a choir loud enough to ring the Deep Bell, and Veyr risen.
- **Fears:** silence. If the bell stops, so does she.

## What she knows

The true history of the Ruin, which the Saltborn half remember and the Guild forgot.
