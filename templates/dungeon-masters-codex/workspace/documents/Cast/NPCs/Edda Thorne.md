Loud, generous with drinks, and sharper than anyone gives her credit for. Edda spotted the pattern in the wrecks years ago and decided to make money from it rather than ask questions.

## Voice and mannerisms

- Laughs first, then talks.
- Stacks and restacks coins while thinking.
- Remembers every debt anyone owes her, to the copper.

## Wants and fears

- **Wants:** her insurance business to keep paying.
- **Fears:** [[Odile Marr|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Odile%20Marr.md]] finding the ledger in the strongroom.

## What she knows

Exactly which ships will vanish next, because they match the pattern: laden with bell metal salvage, sailing on still nights.

## Hooks

- A rival and a red herring. The party may suspect her of causing the wrecks; she only profits from them.

Sits on the council in [[Lanternfall|doc:documents/World/Places/Lanternfall.md]]. Faction: [[the Harbour Compact|doc:documents/Cast/Factions.md#compact]].
