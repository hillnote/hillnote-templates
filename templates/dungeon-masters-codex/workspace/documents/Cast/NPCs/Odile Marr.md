Fifty, weathered, and the most organised person on the coast. Odile hired ${party_name} to find out why the Spire is failing, and she pays on time, which nobody else in Lanternfall does.

## Voice and mannerisms

- Speaks in short, complete sentences. Never says "maybe".
- Taps her ledger with a pencil before refusing anything.
- Calls the party "contractors" until they earn their names.

## Wants and fears

- **Wants:** the truth about the Dark Night, and the light fixed before the winter storms.
- **Fears:** that the Compact will shut the port, and that her brother died for nothing.

## What she knows

- Ships go missing in calm weather, never in storms. She has the dates.
- The Guild refuses every request for an inspection of the Spire.
