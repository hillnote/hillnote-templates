The Compact's best captain and its worst-kept secret. She'll take the party anywhere on the Grey Heron, for a price or a good story.

## Voice and mannerisms

- Swears in ways nobody has heard before.
- Sings shanties badly and loudly in [[The Drowned Bell|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Drowned%20Bell.md]].
- Calls the sea "the old girl".

## Wants and fears

- **Wants:** a fast ship, a full hold, and no questions.
- **Fears:** still water. She's lost two friends to calm nights.

## What she knows

Safe routes through the Teeth, and which crates on the Cinder Quay boats are heavier than lamp oil.

## Hooks

- Gave [[Smoke at Cinder Quay|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/Smoke%20at%20Cinder%20Quay.md]], then quietly hoped it would fail.
- Can sail the party to [[The Lantern Spire|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Lantern%20Spire.md]] for Session 2.

Faction: [[the Harbour Compact|doc:documents/Cast/Factions.md#compact]], with a foot in the Gull Court.
