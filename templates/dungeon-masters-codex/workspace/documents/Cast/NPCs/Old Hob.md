Seventy, toothless, devoted to the flame. Everyone at the Spire treats him as a harmless old man, which is why nobody has noticed what he knows.

## Voice and mannerisms

- Talks to the lamp: "She's hungry tonight."
- Laughs at the wrong moments, then apologises to the flame.
- Counts the hundred and twelve steps out loud every time.

## What he knows

What really goes into the furnace. The second door behind it.

## Hooks

- The key witness for [[The Dark Lighthouse|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/The%20Dark%20Lighthouse.md]]. He will only talk to someone who shows the flame respect, which is Ilse's moment.

Lives at [[The Lantern Spire|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Lantern%20Spire.md]]. Faction: [[the Lamplighters' Guild|doc:documents/Cast/Factions.md#lamplighters]].
