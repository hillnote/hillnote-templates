Ancient, tiny, and frightening in a way that has nothing to do with magic. She has outlived three Guildmasters and doesn't intend to be polite to the fourth.

## Voice and mannerisms

- Whispers, so people lean in.
- Licks salt from her thumb before saying anything important.
- Never uses anyone's name; calls them by what they carry ("the one with the trident").

## Wants and fears

- **Wants:** the city to stay out of the marsh, and the Deep Bell to stay silent.
- **Fears:** the verse of the old song that names the bell.

## What she knows

What the Deep Bell is and where it hangs. How to quiet bell metal with salt.

## Hooks

- Gives [[Bells Beneath the Tide|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/Bells%20Beneath%20the%20Tide.md]] once she trusts the party.
- Sells [[Saltbrew Draught|doc:documents/Bestiary%20and%20Loot/Items/Saltbrew%20Draught.md]].

Somewhere in the reeds near [[Saltmarrow|doc:documents/World/Places/Saltmarrow.md]]. Faction: [[the Saltborn|doc:documents/Cast/Factions.md#saltborn]].
