Kind, patient, beloved by the families she feeds. Her own husband drowned on the Dark Night, and the Choirmother gave him back to her, after a fashion.

## Voice and mannerisms

- Hums hymns under her breath; the tunes are slightly wrong.
- Holds your hand a beat too long when she says goodbye.
- Smells faintly of the sea, even in the abbey kitchen.

## Wants and fears

- **Wants:** every grieving family to hear their drowned sing again.
- **Fears:** Sister Ilse, who trained here and knows the abbey too well.

## What she knows

Who in Lanternfall has joined the Choir, and when the next gathering in the crypt will be.

## Hooks

- Offers the party a warm meal and a sympathetic ear. Takes notes.

Works at the [[Weeping Abbey|doc:documents/World/Places/Weeping%20Abbey.md]]. Faction: [[the Drowned Choir|doc:documents/Cast/Factions.md#choir]], secretly.
