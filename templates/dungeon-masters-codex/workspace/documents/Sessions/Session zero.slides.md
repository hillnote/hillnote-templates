# The Shattered Coast

~

Session zero: what kind of game we're playing, and how we look after each other

***

## The pitch

Twelve years ago the great lighthouse on the Teeth went dark for one night, and three ships broke on the rocks.

Since then the light has dimmed every year. Ships vanish in calm weather. On still nights, the fishermen hear bells under the sea.

You are the people the harbour hires when nobody else will go.

***

## The tone

### Expect

- Mystery first, combat second
- Fog, salt and candlelight
- Villains with reasons
- Choices that cost something

~split

### Don't expect

- Dungeon crawls for their own sake
- Grimdark misery
- Puzzles with one right answer

***

## Safety tools

- **Lines** are things that won't appear in the game at all
- **Veils** can happen, but off screen: we fade to black
- **The X-card:** tap it, or say "X", and we change the scene. No explanation needed
- **Open door:** anyone can step away at any time
- **Stars and wishes** at the end of every session: one thing you loved, one thing you'd like more of

***

## Our lines and veils

| Lines (never in the game) | Veils (off screen) |
|---|---|
| Harm to children | Drowning, in detail |
| Sexual violence | Torture |
| Real-world slurs | Lingering body horror |
| Cruelty to animals | Self-harm |

Add to it any time, privately or out loud. The list only ever grows.

***

## House rules

1. Drinking your own potion is a bonus action
2. Critical hits: maximum damage on the dice, then roll them again and add
3. The GM rolls attacks in the open
4. Inspiration for great in-character moments, one at a time
5. A missing player's character fades into the background, safe, and still earns a share

***

## Your character

- One tie to a faction on the coast
- One secret, shared only with the GM
- A reason to work with the others, decided together tonight

~split

### Factions to tie into

- The Lamplighters' Guild
- The Harbour Compact
- The Saltborn
- The Gull Court

***

## The table

- Fridays, 19:00 to 22:00
- Game on if three of four can play
- A two-minute recap at the start, read from the last session's page
- Phones down, except for the dice roller
