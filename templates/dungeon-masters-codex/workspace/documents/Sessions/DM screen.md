## Setting a difficulty

| Task | DC |
|---|---|
| Easy: most people manage it | 10 |
| Medium: needs some skill | 15 |
| Hard: an expert might fail | 20 |
| Very hard: heroic | 25 |

If failure wouldn't be interesting, don't ask for a roll.

## Improvised damage

| Severity | Level 1 to 4 | Level 5 to 10 |
|---|---|---|

| Dangerous (a collapsing stair, a breaking wave) | 2d10 | 4d10 |
| Deadly (the furnace itself, a fall from the lamp gallery) | 4d10 | 10d10 |
