Priya is away that week, so instead of a short-handed main session, a standalone one-shot for three players using pregenerated Saltborn characters.

## Pitch

It's the Night of the Long Tide in [[Saltmarrow|doc:documents/World/Places/Saltmarrow.md]]: the one night a year the Saltborn light lanterns on the water for their drowned. This year, the drowned light them back.

## Scenes

1. **The festival.** Lanterns, salt bread, and an old song. Someone in the crowd sings a verse they shouldn't know.
2. **The answering lights.** Out on the water, the lanterns are moving against the tide. A [[Lantern Eel|doc:documents/Bestiary%20and%20Loot/Monsters/Lantern%20Eel.md]] and its "lights".
3. **The singer.** A grieving widow recruited by the Choir. Talk her down, or chase her into the reeds.

## Why it matters

It shows the Saltborn side of the story and plants the verse that names the Deep Bell, so the main party can hear about it later as a rumour.
