A sketch, to be firmed up after session 2. Where it goes depends on what the party finds at the Spire.

## If they open the second door

They are in the sea caves under the Spire with the tide coming in. Run the first two rooms of [[The Sunken Stair|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Sunken%20Stair.md]]: the cave mouth and the Weeping Steps, with brinewights. They hear the Choir for the first time.

## If they confront Corvin

He doesn't fight. He pours tea and explains, and asks them what they would do instead. If they have no answer, he asks them to keep his secret until the winter storms are over. This is Marcus's moral choice for the session.

## If they leave the Spire with proof

[[Odile Marr|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Odile%20Marr.md]] wants to storm the Spire with Compact marines. The party has to choose sides, and [[Edda Thorne|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Edda%20Thorne.md]] starts asking about them.

## Either way

- Mother Ysa sends for them: someone has been singing the old verse in Saltmarrow.
- Tick the Choir's clock on the [[Campaign arc|doc:documents/Quests/Campaign%20arc.md]].
