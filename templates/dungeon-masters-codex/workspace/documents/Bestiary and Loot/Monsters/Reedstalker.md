**Skills** Stealth +5, Perception +4 · **Senses** passive Perception 14

**Traits**

- ***Still as Reeds.*** While it doesn't move, the reedstalker has advantage on Stealth checks in marsh or reeds.
- ***Ambush.*** In the first round of combat, its attacks against a surprised creature score a critical hit on a 19 or 20.

**Actions**

- ***Spear Beak.*** Melee attack: +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage.
- ***Pin (bonus action).*** After a beak hit, the target makes a DC 12 Strength save or is knocked prone.

A heron the height of a horse, with a mantis's patience. Its long neck folds back into its shoulders until it looks like a dead tree.

## Tactics

Waits motionless, strikes the last person in line, then retreats into deep reeds after one bite. Comes back when the party splits up. Hunts in pairs during the autumn tides.
