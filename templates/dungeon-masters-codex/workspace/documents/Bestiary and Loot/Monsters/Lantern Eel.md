**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11

**Traits**

- ***Lure Light.*** The eel's head glows like a ship's lantern. A creature that starts its turn within 60 feet and can see the light makes a DC 12 Wisdom save or uses its movement to approach it. On a success it is immune for 24 hours.
- ***Water Breathing.*** The eel can breathe only underwater.

**Actions**

- ***Bite.*** Melee attack: +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage.
- ***Shock Pulse (recharge 5–6).*** Each creature in water within 15 feet makes a DC 13 Constitution save, taking 14 (4d6) lightning damage on a failure, or half on a success.

## Tactics

Lures a lookout or a swimmer close, bites, then pulses once several people are in the water. Flees to the depths if its light is covered: a cloak thrown over its head, or magical darkness.

## In the story

Lantern eels have multiplied around the Teeth since the Spire dimmed. Sailors mistake their light for the lighthouse, which is exactly how a ship ends up on the rocks. One lives in the sea caves under [[The Lantern Spire|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Lantern%20Spire.md]].
