**Resistances** cold · **Immunities** poison · **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10 · **Languages** understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak

**Traits**

- ***Undertow Grip.*** A creature hit by the brinewight's slam is grappled (escape DC 12). While grappled underwater, it can't breathe.
- ***Salt-Soaked.*** The brinewight takes half damage from fire, and on land it leaves a trail of seawater that anyone can follow.

**Actions**

- ***Multiattack.*** Two slams.
- ***Slam.*** Melee attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage.
- ***Retch Brine (recharge 5–6).*** Each creature in a 15-foot cone makes a DC 13 Constitution save. On a failure it takes 10 (3d6) cold damage and is poisoned until the end of its next turn; on a success, half damage.

Sailors from the wrecked ships, raised by the Choir to row its boats and guard its stair. They still wear what they drowned in.

## Tactics

Fight in pairs. One grapples and drags its target into deeper water; the other retches brine at anyone wading in to help. Below 10 hit points, a brinewight sinks out of reach and comes back on the next tide.

## In the story

On the Weeping Steps of [[The Sunken Stair|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Sunken%20Stair.md]], and around the Teeth on still nights. Brakka may recognise a face from the Dark Night.
