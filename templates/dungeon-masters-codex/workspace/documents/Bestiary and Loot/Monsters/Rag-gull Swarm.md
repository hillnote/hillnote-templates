*Medium swarm of Tiny beasts*

| Armour Class | Hit Points | Speed | Challenge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 22 (5d8) | 10 ft., fly 40 ft. | 1/2 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 (−2) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 3 (−4) | 12 (+1) | 6 (−2) |

**Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing · **Senses** passive Perception 11

**Traits**

- ***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and move through any opening large enough for a gull. It can't regain hit points.
- ***Thieving Flock.*** When the swarm hits, roll a d6. On a 6 it snatches one small, unsecured item: a pouch, a vial, a key.

**Actions**

- ***Beaks.*** Melee attack: +4 to hit, reach 0 ft., one creature in its space. Hit: 7 (2d6) piercing damage, or 3 (1d6) if the swarm has half its hit points or fewer.

## Tactics

Harass, steal, scatter. The swarm goes for whoever is carrying something shiny and flees the moment it takes area damage. The Gull Court trains them to lift purses on the quayside.

The party met a swarm in [[Session 1 - The Drowned Bell|doc:documents/Sessions/Sessions/Session%201%20-%20The%20Drowned%20Bell.md]], when it stole Tamsin's notes outside [[The Drowned Bell|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Drowned%20Bell.md]]. Common at [[Cinder Quay|doc:documents/World/Places/Cinder%20Quay.md]] and anywhere [[Pell Rook|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Pell%20Rook.md]] wants to make a point.
