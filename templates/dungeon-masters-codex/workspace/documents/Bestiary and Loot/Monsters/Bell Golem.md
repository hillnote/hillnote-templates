**Immunities** poison, psychic; charmed, frightened, poisoned · **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 10

**Traits**

- ***Resonant Body.*** Whenever the golem takes thunder damage, it regains that many hit points instead.
- ***Muffled.*** A creature adjacent to the golem can use its action to pack the bell mouth with salt or cloth (DC 13 Athletics). The golem can't use Toll until it spends an action shaking itself clear.

**Actions**

- ***Multiattack.*** Two slams.
- ***Slam.*** Melee attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
- ***Toll (recharge 5–6).*** Each creature within 20 feet makes a DC 14 Constitution save, taking 18 (4d8) thunder damage and being deafened until the end of its next turn on a failure, or half damage on a success.

A Veyran temple bell on legs, green with age, that walks on its rim and rings when it hits you.

## Tactics

Stands in a doorway and doesn't move. Tolls as soon as three or more creatures are in range. It is far too tough for a level-${party_level} party to beat in a straight fight: the trick is the salt. If the party has met [[Mother Ysa|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Mother%20Ysa.md]], they know it.

## In the story

Two stand in the Bell Gallery of [[The Sunken Stair|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Sunken%20Stair.md]].
