**Skills** Deception +4, Religion +2 · **Senses** passive Perception 11 · **Languages** Common, a little Old Veyran

**Traits**

- ***Choir Harmony.*** The cultist has advantage on attack rolls while another cultist within 10 feet is singing. Singing takes a bonus action and lasts until the start of the singer's next turn.
- ***Gift of Gills.*** The cultist can breathe underwater.

**Actions**

- ***Hooked Knife.*** Melee attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage. If the target is in water, it is pulled 5 feet towards the cultist.
- ***Drowning Hymn (1/day).*** One creature within 30 feet that can hear the cultist makes a DC 12 Constitution save. On a failure it can't speak and is poisoned for 1 minute, repeating the save at the end of each of its turns.

## Tactics

Never alone. They sing first and stab second, and they retreat into the water to wait. Captured cultists are grieving neighbours, not fanatics, and can be talked down with news of their drowned.

## In the story

Recruited by [[Sister Maudlin Greaves|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Sister%20Maudlin%20Greaves.md]] at the [[Weeping Abbey|doc:documents/World/Places/Weeping%20Abbey.md]]. They guard the Choir Loft in [[The Sunken Stair|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Sunken%20Stair.md]].
