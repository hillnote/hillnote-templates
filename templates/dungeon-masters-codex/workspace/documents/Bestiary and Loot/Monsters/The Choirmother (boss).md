**Saving throws** Wis +6, Cha +8 · **Resistances** cold, thunder · **Immunities** charmed, frightened · **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 13 · **Languages** Common, Old Veyran

**Traits**

- ***Two Voices.*** She can concentrate on two effects at once.
- ***Echo of the Deep Bell.*** While the Deep Bell rings, she can't be reduced below 1 hit point except by radiant damage. If the bell is silenced, she loses this trait and her current hit points are halved.
- ***Chorus (3/round).*** At the end of another creature's turn she can use one Chorus option: **Hum**, one creature within 30 feet takes 7 (2d6) thunder damage; **Call**, one charmed creature moves 15 feet towards deep water; **Harmonise**, she regains 10 hit points if a cultist is within 30 feet.

**Actions**

- ***Multiattack.*** Two Coral Talon attacks, or one talon and Drowning Note.
- ***Coral Talon.*** Melee attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d10 + 4) slashing damage.
- ***Drowning Note.*** One creature within 60 feet makes a DC 16 Constitution save, taking 21 (6d6) cold damage and losing its voice until the end of its next turn on a failure, or half damage on a success.
- ***Song of the Deep (recharge 5–6).*** Each creature within 30 feet that can hear her makes a DC 16 Wisdom save or is charmed for 1 minute, using its movement each turn to walk towards the nearest deep water. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns.

The battle form of [[The Choirmother|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/The%20Choirmother.md]]: the final fight of the campaign, built for a party of about level 8.

## Tactics

She never fights alone; cultists and brinewights come first. She opens with Song of the Deep, uses Call to pull charmed characters into the water, and focuses whoever is carrying a light. Once the Deep Bell is silenced she becomes desperate and reckless, and very beatable.

## In the story

The Chamber of the Deep Bell, at the bottom of [[The Sunken Stair|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Sunken%20Stair.md]]. The way to win is to silence the bell, which is what [[Mother Ysa|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Mother%20Ysa.md]]'s song is for.
