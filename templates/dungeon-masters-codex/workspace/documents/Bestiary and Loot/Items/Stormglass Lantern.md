A brass lantern with a shard of stormglass where the wick should be. It burns cold and never goes out.

## Properties

- While attuned, you see invisible creatures and the spirits of the drowned within 30 feet of the lantern.
- **Flare (once per dawn).** As an action, each creature of your choice within 15 feet makes a DC 14 Constitution save or is blinded until the end of your next turn.

## History

Made by the Lamplighters from the first stormglass ever brought up. [[Corvin Hale|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Corvin%20Hale.md]] carries it everywhere, and it is the one thing that lets him see the Choirmother's messengers coming.
