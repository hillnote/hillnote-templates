A curved piece of green bronze as long as a forearm, engraved with drowned script. It hums.

## Properties

- While attuned, you can hear the Drowned Choir from up to a mile away, and you always know the direction of the nearest Veyran bell.
- Three shards held together can ring a single note that silences any bell, the Deep Bell included, for 1 minute.

## History

The Deep Bell cracked when it was rung at the Ruin, and its shards scattered across the coast. One rests in [[The Sunken Stair|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Sunken%20Stair.md]]. Tamsin secretly owns another. Where is the third? That's a side quest waiting to happen.
