A wax-sealed writ from the harbourmaster's office.

## Properties

- Opens the harbour gates and the Compact warehouses in [[Lanternfall|doc:documents/World/Places/Lanternfall.md]].
- Once, the bearer may commandeer a Compact rowing boat and crew.

## History

Issued by [[Odile Marr|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Odile%20Marr.md]] when she hired the party. Worthless to sell, priceless to have.
