A cloudy grey potion that tastes like the sea.

## Properties

Drinking it ends one disease or the poisoned condition. For 1 hour afterwards, you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed by music or song.

## History

The Saltborn brew it against the Choir's hymns. [[Mother Ysa|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Mother%20Ysa.md]] sells it, if she likes you.
