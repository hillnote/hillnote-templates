A grey cloak sewn with gull feathers, smelling faintly of fish.

## Properties

- Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in fog, darkness or crowds.
- Once per dawn, when you fall, you descend at 60 feet per round and take no falling damage.

## History

Every lieutenant of the Gull Court gets one. [[Pell Rook|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Pell%20Rook.md]]'s is the original. Wren wants one more than she wants her debt cleared.
