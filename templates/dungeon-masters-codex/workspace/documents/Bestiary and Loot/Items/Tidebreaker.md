A Compact marine's trident with a bronze head shaped like breaking surf.

## Properties

- +1 to attack and damage rolls.
- On a critical hit against a creature standing in water, the target is pushed 10 feet and knocked prone.

## History

Brakka's from her marine days. She pulled it out of the water on the Dark Night and has never been able to say how it got there.
