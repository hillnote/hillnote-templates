## Hook

Nell Dross, the ferryman's daughter, begged help at [[The Drowned Bell|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Drowned%20Bell.md]]: her father hasn't come home in three days.

## What happened

The party tracked [[Fenwick Dross|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Fenwick%20Dross.md]]'s boat to a sandbar off the Teeth and found him half-drowned and singing, with brinewight tracks in the sand around him.

Complete in [[Session 1 - The Drowned Bell|doc:documents/Sessions/Sessions/Session%201%20-%20The%20Drowned%20Bell.md]]. Fenwick ferries the party free for life, and his story pointed them at the bells.
