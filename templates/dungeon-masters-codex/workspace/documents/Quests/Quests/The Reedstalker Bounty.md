## Hook

A notice at [[The Drowned Bell|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Drowned%20Bell.md]]: "120 gp for the beak of the thing taking herders' goats at the edge of Hollowmere. Ask for Mags."

## Twist

There are two [[reedstalkers|doc:documents/Bestiary%20and%20Loot/Monsters/Reedstalker.md]], nesting by a half-sunk Veyran watchtower in [[The Drowned Wood|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Drowned%20Wood.md]]. The tower's bell still has its clapper.

## Rewards

120 gp, and a reason to visit the watchtower. Ringing that bell would be a very bad idea.
