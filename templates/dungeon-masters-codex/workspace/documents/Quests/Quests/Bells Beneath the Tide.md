## Hook

Fishermen in [[Saltmarrow|doc:documents/World/Places/Saltmarrow.md]] hear bells under the water on still nights. [[Fenwick Dross|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Fenwick%20Dross.md]] heard his own name in the singing.

## The truth

The bells are the Drowned Choir, and the song is rising. [[Mother Ysa|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Mother%20Ysa.md]] knows what it means.

## Steps

1. Earn Mother Ysa's trust (bring salt, see [[Salt for Saltmarrow|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/Salt%20for%20Saltmarrow.md]]).
2. Learn the verse that names the Deep Bell.
3. Find the way down: the sea caves under [[The Lantern Spire|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Lantern%20Spire.md]].

## Rewards

The Saltborn as allies, and the song that can silence the Deep Bell.
