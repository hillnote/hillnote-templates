## Hook

[[Pell Rook|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Pell%20Rook.md]] has called in Wren's childhood debt: 200 gp, or one job, no questions asked.

## The job

Collect a crate from a rowing boat off the Teeth at midnight and bring it to the Spire's jetty. The crate is stormglass. Pell doesn't know what it is, and Wren is about to find out.

## Choices

- Do the job, clear the debt, and learn where the stormglass comes from.
- Refuse, and make an enemy of the Court.
- Tell Pell the truth, and maybe turn the Gull Court against the Guild.
