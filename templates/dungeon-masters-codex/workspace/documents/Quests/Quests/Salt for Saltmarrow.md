## Hook

The autumn tides flooded [[Saltmarrow|doc:documents/World/Places/Saltmarrow.md]]'s salt pans. Without salt, the winter fish will rot. The reeve asks the party to bring three cartloads from [[Lanternfall|doc:documents/World/Places/Lanternfall.md]] before Stormmonth.

## Complications

- The Compact's salt merchants have doubled their prices.
- The marsh road is watched, and something has been taking carts.

## Rewards

The village's gratitude and, more importantly, an introduction to [[Mother Ysa|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Mother%20Ysa.md]], which opens [[Bells Beneath the Tide|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/Bells%20Beneath%20the%20Tide.md]].
