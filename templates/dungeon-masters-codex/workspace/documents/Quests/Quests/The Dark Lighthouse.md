## Hook

[[Odile Marr|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Odile%20Marr.md]] wants to know why the Spire is failing and why the Guild refuses every inspection. She has hired ${party_name} to get inside and find out.

## The truth

The flame is fed with stormglass bought from below with wrecked ships. See the [[Campaign bible|doc:documents/World/Campaign%20bible.md]].

## Scenes

1. **Arrival.** [[Corvin Hale|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Corvin%20Hale.md]] welcomes the party with tea and a guided tour that avoids the furnace room.
2. **The keepers' hall at night.** [[Old Hob|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Old%20Hob.md]] talks to the lamp. If someone shows the flame respect, he talks to them too.
3. **The furnace room.** Stormglass, a sealed second door, and something wet on the floor.

## Complications

- The Guildmaster is not a monster, and the party may like him.
