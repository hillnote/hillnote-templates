> The cliff path ends in a cove full of smoke. Boats with no names, fires in old barrels, and a hundred gulls perched along the rocks, watching you like customs officers.

## What's here

- **The Rookery**, a wrecked ship hauled ashore and turned into [[Pell Rook|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Pell%20Rook.md]]'s hall.
- **The drying racks**, which hide the real cargo under the fish.
- **The tunnel** up to [[Lanternfall|doc:documents/World/Places/Lanternfall.md]]'s harbour wall.

## Who's here

[[Pell Rook|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Pell%20Rook.md]] and the Gull Court.

## Secrets

The Court has been ferrying crates of stormglass from the Teeth to the Spire for the Guildmaster. Pell thinks it is untaxed lamp oil. The party nearly caught a shipment in [[Smoke at Cinder Quay|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/Smoke%20at%20Cinder%20Quay.md]].

## Travel

Two hours up the cliff path to [[Lanternfall|doc:documents/World/Places/Lanternfall.md]], or half a day by boat to the Teeth.
