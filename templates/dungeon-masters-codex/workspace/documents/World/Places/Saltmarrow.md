> Houses on stilts above grey water, joined by rope bridges. Salt pans glitter between the reeds. Nobody here speaks above a murmur after dark, and every boat has a little iron bell nailed to the bow, clapper removed.

## What's here

- **The ferry landing**, where [[Fenwick Dross|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Fenwick%20Dross.md]] went missing (Session 1).
- **The salt pans**, flooded by the autumn tides, which is a problem: see [[Salt for Saltmarrow|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/Salt%20for%20Saltmarrow.md]].
- **Mother Ysa's hut**, an hour into the reeds, if she wants to be found.

## Who's here

[[Fenwick Dross|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Fenwick%20Dross.md]], back home and shaken. [[Mother Ysa|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Mother%20Ysa.md]], somewhere in the reeds.

## Secrets

The Saltborn remember the Ruin in their songs. One verse is the true name of the Deep Bell. They will not sing it to outsiders, and they are right not to.
