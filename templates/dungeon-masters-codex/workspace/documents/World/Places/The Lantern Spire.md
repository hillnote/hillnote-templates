> It rises from the largest of the Teeth like a finger raised for silence: three hundred feet of black stone, and at the top a flame the colour of cold honey. Up close you can hear it. It sounds like breathing.

## What's here

- **The jetty and the keepers' hall**, where [[Corvin Hale|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Corvin%20Hale.md]] receives visitors with tea.
- **The furnace room**, where stormglass is fed to the flame. Off limits.
- **The lamp gallery**, a hundred and twelve steps up.
- **The lower vaults**, cut into the rock below the tide line. They lead to the sea caves, and the sea caves lead down to [[The Sunken Stair|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Sunken%20Stair.md]].

## Who's here

[[Corvin Hale|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Corvin%20Hale.md]], [[Old Hob|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Old%20Hob.md]], and a dozen keepers who believe every word the Guildmaster says.

## Secrets

The flame is fed with stormglass from below, and the stormglass is paid for in ships. The furnace has a second door, sealed with bell metal, that opens onto the sea caves.
