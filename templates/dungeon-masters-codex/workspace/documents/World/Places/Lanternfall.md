> The fog lifts off the harbour like a curtain. Above the masts, the town climbs the chalk in crooked terraces, every door with a candle burning in a niche beside it. Somewhere a bell is ringing the hour, and the gulls are louder than the market.

## What's here

- **The harbour and the counting-house.** Compact ships, Compact marines, and [[Odile Marr|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Odile%20Marr.md]]'s office with its view of every mast.
- **[[The Drowned Bell|doc:documents/World/Places/The%20Drowned%20Bell.md]]**, the tavern where Session 1 began.
- **Lamp Street.** Every house keeps a candle lit for the Lantern; an unlit niche means a death in the family.
- **The Compact strongroom**, under the counting-house.

## Who's here

[[Odile Marr|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Odile%20Marr.md]], [[Edda Thorne|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Edda%20Thorne.md]] and [[Captain Rhoswen Kroy|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Captain%20Rhoswen%20Kroy.md]].

## Secrets

- [[Edda Thorne|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Edda%20Thorne.md]]'s insurance ledger is in the strongroom. It lists ships she insured heavily the week before they vanished.
- A smugglers' tunnel runs from the harbour wall down to [[Cinder Quay|doc:documents/World/Places/Cinder%20Quay.md]].
