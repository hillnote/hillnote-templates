> The trees are grey and smooth as driftwood, and they make no sound when the wind moves through them. There are barnacles halfway up the trunks. Something tall stands very still among the reeds, and then it isn't there.

## What's here

- **A Veyran watchtower**, half sunk, with a bell still in it.
- **Reedstalker nests** in the flooded clearings. See [[The Reedstalker Bounty|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/The%20Reedstalker%20Bounty.md]].
- **Saltborn trail markers**: knotted reeds that mean "turn back".

## Who's here

Nobody, officially. [[Reedstalkers|doc:documents/Bestiary%20and%20Loot/Monsters/Reedstalker.md]], and the occasional Saltborn hunter.

## Secrets

The watchtower bell still has its clapper. Ringing it would be heard below, and answered.

## Travel

Two days through the reeds from [[Saltmarrow|doc:documents/World/Places/Saltmarrow.md]], with a guide. Without one, roll for getting lost every half day.
