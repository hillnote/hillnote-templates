> The abbey hangs on the cliff edge, white stone streaked with salt. In the courtyard a statue of Saint Orlaith holds up a lamp, and from her stone eyes run two thin lines of seawater that never dry.

## What's here

- **The training hall**, where Sister Ilse took her vows.
- **The statue of Saint Orlaith**, founder of the Lamplighters, which began weeping salt water twelve years ago.
- **The crypt**, officially closed for repairs.

## Who's here

[[Sister Maudlin Greaves|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Sister%20Maudlin%20Greaves.md]] runs the abbey's mercy kitchen for the families of the drowned.

## Secrets

The Drowned Choir meets in the crypt. Maudlin recruits grieving families with the promise that their drowned will sing again, and they will. The statue began weeping on the Dark Night.

## Hooks

- Ilse's old teacher asks her, quietly, why the crypt is really closed.
- A grieving widow from Lamp Street has stopped lighting her candle and started singing.
