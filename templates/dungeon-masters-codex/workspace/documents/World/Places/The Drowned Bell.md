> Low beams, wet boots, and a bronze bell the size of a barrel hanging over the bar. The landlady swears it was pulled from the sea in her grandmother's day. On still nights it hums.

## What's here

- **Mags Penhallow**, landlady: knows every captain's debts and every sailor's widow.
- **The notice board**: work for hire, including [[The Reedstalker Bounty|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/The%20Reedstalker%20Bounty.md]].
- **The bell.** It is real Veyran bell metal. It hums when the Choir sings below, louder each week.

## Who's here

Sailors, Compact clerks, off-duty marines, and on most nights [[Captain Rhoswen Kroy|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Captain%20Rhoswen%20Kroy.md]] singing badly.

## Secrets

The bell hums in time with the bells under the sea. Anyone who sleeps in the room above it dreams of drowned towers. Tamsin should be the one to notice.
