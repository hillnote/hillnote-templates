## The pitch

> Twelve years ago, the great lighthouse on the Teeth went dark for one night, and three ships broke on the rocks. Since then the light has burned a little dimmer every year. Ships vanish in calm weather. On still nights, the fishermen of Saltmarrow hear bells ringing under the sea. The Harbour Compact of Lanternfall has had enough, and it is hiring.

**The Shattered Coast** is a nautical-gothic campaign about light, debt and what a city will pay to stay safe. It runs from level 1 to about level 8 over roughly twenty sessions.

## Tone

| Lean into | Steer away from |
|---|---|
| Mystery first, combat second | Dungeon crawls for their own sake |
| Fog, salt, candlelight, the creak of ropes | Grimdark misery and hopeless odds |
| NPCs with understandable motives, even villains | Evil for its own sake |
| Choices with costs and no perfect answer | Puzzles with one right solution |

Three words for every scene: **damp, candlelit, uneasy**.

## Themes

1. **The cost of safety.** The light keeps ships safe, and someone is paying for it in a currency nobody agreed to.
2. **The sea remembers.** The drowned empire of Veyr is gone, not finished.
3. **Debts come due.** Personal, financial, ancestral. Every faction owes or is owed.

## Truths of the coast

These are true whatever the players believe. Tell them as rumours, signs and slips of the tongue, never as exposition.

1. The coast broke 1,192 years ago when the sea-kings of Veyr rang the Deep Bell to command the tide, and the tide answered. Years are counted After the Ruin (AR).
2. The Lantern Spire's flame is fed with **stormglass**, crystals of hardened sea-light mined from the drowned city. Without it the light dies.
3. The stormglass is running out.
4. Bell metal from Veyr still hums, and anything that hums can be heard below.
5. The drowned don't always stay drowned.
6. Three gods are worshipped on the coast: **the Lantern** (guidance and safe harbour), **Old Brine** (the hungry sea and storms) and **the Quiet Lady** (death, mercy and the drowned).

## What's really going on

Guildmaster [[Corvin Hale|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/Corvin%20Hale.md]] found the last seam of stormglass twelve years ago, on the night the light went out. Below it waited [[The Choirmother|doc:documents/Cast/NPCs/The%20Choirmother.md]], a priestess of Veyr kept alive by the Deep Bell's echo. She offered a trade: stormglass from below for ships on the rocks above. Corvin believes he is choosing a few ships over a dark coast and a thousand wrecks.

The Choirmother wants something else entirely. Each wreck brings her bell metal and souls for her choir. When the choir is loud enough, she will ring the Deep Bell again and raise Veyr, and Lanternfall will drown in its place.

The players will learn this in three steps: the light is being fed ([[The Dark Lighthouse|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/The%20Dark%20Lighthouse.md]]), the bells are a choir ([[Bells Beneath the Tide|doc:documents/Quests/Quests/Bells%20Beneath%20the%20Tide.md]]), and the Guildmaster made the bargain. The [[Campaign arc|doc:documents/Quests/Campaign%20arc.md]] tracks how close the Choirmother is.
