Margin of error:

Uses the same confidence level, proportion and population as above.

How it works

n₀ = z² × p × (1 − p) ÷ e²  ·  n = n₀ ÷ (1 + (n₀ − 1) ÷ N)  ·  invitations = n ÷ response rate

z comes from the confidence level (1.96 for 95%), p is the expected proportion, e the margin of error and N the population. The finite population correction only matters when the sample is a sizeable share of the population. This assumes a simple random sample; clustered or quota samples need more. Interview studies size themselves differently: plan for saturation, not a margin of error.
