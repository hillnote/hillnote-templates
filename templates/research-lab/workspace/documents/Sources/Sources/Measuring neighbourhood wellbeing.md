## Summary

From the abstract: develops and validates a nine-item neighbourhood wellbeing scale in two cities, covering trust, belonging, safety and satisfaction with local spaces.

## Why it's on the list

A candidate instrument for the optional phase 2 survey in the research plan. A short, validated scale would make the results comparable with other studies.

## Questions to read for

- How reliable is it (internal consistency, test-retest)?
- Can it be used freely, and in what form?
- Do any items assume home ownership? That would bias it against renters.
