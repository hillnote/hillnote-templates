## Summary

From the abstract: 140 gardeners wore accelerometers for two weeks. On days they visited the garden, they did about 40 more minutes of moderate activity than on other days.

## Why it's on the list

Objective activity data, rather than self-report, for RQ1. It is a conference paper, so first check whether a fuller journal version exists.

## Questions to read for
