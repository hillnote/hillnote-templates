**Sources:** [[Ramírez-Cole (2022)|doc:documents/Sources/Sources/Waiting%20for%20a%20plot.md]], interviews P02 and P05

**Evidence:** a median wait of three and a half years; residency rules and plots passed to relatives keep renters and newcomers out. Two of five interviewees independently described giving up on the waiting list.

**Why it contradicts H1:** if access is rationed by tenure, benefits flow to settled owners, and that is not "the whole neighbourhood".
