**Source:** [[Hartmann, Osei and Brandt (2019)|doc:documents/Sources/Sources/Green%20prescriptions.md]]

**Evidence:** new plot holders scored about 0.3 SD higher on wellbeing than waiting-list controls after twelve months, and the gap held at three years. The effect was largest among people living alone.

**Caveats:** more controls than gardeners dropped out. This is about gardeners, not the wider neighbourhood, so it supports the "gardens are good for gardeners" half of H1 only.
