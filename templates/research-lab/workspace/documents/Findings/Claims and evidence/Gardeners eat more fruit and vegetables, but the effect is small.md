**Source:** [[Mbeki, Sørensen and Liu (2020)|doc:documents/Sources/Sources/Harvest%20and%20health.md]]

**Evidence:** about 0.6 more portions of fruit and vegetables a day across 14 studies. The better-controlled studies show smaller effects.

**Caveats:** nine of the 14 studies were at high risk of bias. Moderate is generous; it would drop to Weak if the review were updated with the same results.
