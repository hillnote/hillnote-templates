# Monthly report template

Duplicate this page at the start of each month. A report answers three questions: what happened, why, and what we're doing about it. Keep it to one screen.

## Headline

One sentence: the number that matters most, against its target, and the reason.

## Campaigns

For each live campaign: on track, at risk or off track, and one line on why. Pacing comes from the **Campaigns** database.

## Content

What shipped, what performed and what didn't. Link the rows in the **Content calendar**.

## What we learned

Three bullets at most. A learning changes what we do next month; if it doesn't, it's an observation.
