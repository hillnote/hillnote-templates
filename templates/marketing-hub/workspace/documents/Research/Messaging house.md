# Messaging house

One page that every piece of copy is checked against. The roof is the single idea, the pillars hold it up, and the foundation makes it believable. If a headline can't be traced back to this page, it doesn't ship.

## The roof

> **${product} is the CRM that does the follow-up for you.**

For agencies and consultancies of 10 to 50 people who lose deals between the pitch and the signature, ${product} keeps every lead moving with automations that need no setup, so the founder stops being the pipeline.

## Pillars

| | Never drop a lead | Set up in an afternoon | See what's coming |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Message** | ${feature} follows up, nudges and reassigns, so nothing sits still | Templates built for agencies; import the spreadsheet and go | A 90-day forecast you can trust, because the pipeline is always current |
| **Proof** | Beta agencies replied to new leads 2.4 times faster | Median setup time in the beta: 3 hours | 8 of 10 beta founders now forecast from ${product} |
| **Features** | ${feature} rules, follow-up sequences, stale-deal alerts | Spreadsheet import, agency pipeline templates | Forecast view, weighted pipeline |

## Boilerplate

**25 words:** ${product} is the CRM for agencies that does the follow-up for you. ${feature} keeps every lead moving, with no setup and no admin.

**50 words:** ${product} is the CRM for agencies and consultancies that does the follow-up for you. ${feature} sends the follow-ups, flags deals that have gone quiet and moves the pipeline along on its own, so founders stop carrying it in their heads. Set up in an afternoon, from $${price_per_seat} a seat.

## Words we use, words we avoid

| Use | Avoid | Because |
|---|---|---|
| follow-up | engagement | It's the word customers use |
| set up in an afternoon | seamless onboarding | Specific beats vague |
| automations | AI-powered | Show what it does instead of starting an argument |
| agencies and consultancies | SMBs | Nobody calls themselves an SMB |
| deals | opportunities | Agency people say deals |
